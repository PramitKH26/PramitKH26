## Featured projects

**[Saul Goodman Courtroom](https://saul-goodman-courtroom-nu.vercel.app)** (live demo)
Two lawyer agents argue a case in front of a deterministic rulebook judge, with a hash-chained,
replayable event log and a game-theory lab: payoff matrices, exact Nash equilibria, best-response
search, minimax/alpha-beta and Pareto analysis. CS F407 course project, Team 22.
*Python, FastAPI, JavaScript.*

**Chart Crimes**
A controlled benchmark of misleading charts for vision-language models (Qwen2.5-VL 3B and 7B), with
a human baseline and LoRA fine-tuning arms on the BITS Sharanga HPC cluster.
